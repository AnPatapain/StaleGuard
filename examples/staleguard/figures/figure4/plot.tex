\caption{Unsafe flight rate (left) and safe mission-completion rate (right)
versus approval-relative dispatch delay for Inspection Descent
(276 scenarios; top), Geofence Turn (300 scenarios; middle), and
Underpass Inspection (400 scenarios; bottom). Each tested delay uses all
scenarios in that mission; lines connect sampled delays. Inspection and
Geofence cover 0--1\,s using 11 and 23 delays, respectively; Underpass
covers 0--2\,s using 13 delays. The dashed gray lines mark Underpass's
unchanged 1\,s prediction horizon. Command-only EarlyRecovery and
Fixed EarlyRecovery (0.20\,s) are shown where evaluated. StaleGuard (full),
the three-profile DualHorizon policy, was evaluated only on Underpass;
its curves coincide with the RecoveryAware ablation, which is omitted
for clarity. Dispatch-only StaleGuard and command-only EarlyRecovery
retain their original definitions.}
\label{fig:policy_curves_3x2}
